Empowering Physiotherapy with Modern Technology

Progress Update

October 2025

The Challenge

Current physiotherapy provision is economically unfeasible for most businesses, creating a care gap that results in preventable workplace injuries, costly sick leave, and overburdened NHS services.

Pain Points

  • Businesses can't afford dedicated physiotherapists
  • Reactive care only - injuries treated too late
  • Preventable accidents from unidentified vulnerabilities
  • Extended sick leave overwhelming businesses and NHS
  • No ongoing care between sporadic physio visits

The Need

  • Digital-first approach
  • Secure, affordable, scalable physiotherapy for all employees
  • Proactive screening to prevent workplace injuries
  • Continuous care reducing NHS referrals
  • Data-driven insights lowering absenteeism costs

Our Solution

A secure, multi-tenant SaaS platform that modernises physiotherapy practice from assessment to recovery.

Digital Assessments

Structured, repeatable patient evaluations with instant scoring

Workout Management

Video-guided exercises with progress tracking

Multi-Tenant Architecture

Security-First Approach

Healthcare data requires the highest standards. We're building security in from day one.

Multi-Tenant Isolation

  • Row-Level Security (RLS)
  • Tenant ID in every query
  • JWT claims validation
  • Zero cross-tenant access

Data Protection

  • Encryption at rest (AES256)
  • TLS 1.3 in transit
  • AWS KMS key management
  • Audit logging planned
